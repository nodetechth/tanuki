Welcome to Tanuki listening practice.

Today we will practice clear business English with a short story.
Imagine you are joining a product meeting on Monday morning. Your manager says, "We need a realistic plan before Friday, and we need everyone to understand the trade-offs."

The team discusses three options. The first option is fast, but it may create quality problems later. The second option is reliable, but it will take more time. The third option is creative, but nobody has tested it yet.

Your task is to listen carefully, notice the speaker's rhythm, and repeat each sentence with natural stress and intonation.
